Weight Loss Yoga FAQs

Can yoga help with weight loss?

Yoga can support an active and healthy lifestyle. Weight management usually depends on several factors, including nutrition, physical activity, sleep and overall energy balance.

Can yoga reduce belly fat?

Yoga can help improve overall fitness, strength, mobility and physical activity. However, it is not possible to selectively burn fat from only one area of the body through a particular exercise.
