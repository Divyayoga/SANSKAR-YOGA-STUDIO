Frequently Asked Questions

Before You Start

Can beginners join online yoga classes?

Yes. Beginners can start with simple practices and progress gradually according to their comfort and experience.

Do you offer one-to-one personal yoga classes?

Yes. One-to-one online sessions are available for students who want more personalized guidance.

Can I practice yoga from outside India?

Yes. Online sessions can be attended from anywhere with a stable internet connection. Class timing can be discussed according to availability.

Do I need to be flexible before starting yoga?

No. Flexibility is something that can develop gradually through appropriate practice. You do not need to be flexible to begin.
