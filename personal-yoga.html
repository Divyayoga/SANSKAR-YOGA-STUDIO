What equipment do I need?

Usually a yoga mat and enough comfortable space to move are sufficient. Additional equipment may be suggested depending on your practice.

Can personal yoga help with weight management?

Yoga can be part of an active and healthy lifestyle. A personalized practice may include movement, strength, mobility and breathing practices. Sustainable weight management also depends on nutrition, activity, sleep and other lifestyle factors.

Is prenatal or postnatal yoga available?

Prenatal and postnatal practices require appropriate modifications and individual consideration. Please contact Mukesh before booking so the suitable practice can be discussed.
