Important Safety Information

Your Safety Comes First

Pregnancy and postpartum recovery are individual experiences. Yoga should always be adapted to the person's health, recovery and professional medical guidance.

Please Read Before Booking

Prenatal and postnatal yoga classes are intended as wellness and movement guidance and are not a substitute for medical care, diagnosis or treatment.


If you are pregnant, recently gave birth, have had a complicated pregnancy or delivery, or have any medical concerns, consult your qualified healthcare professional before beginning or changing your exercise routine.


During practice, never force a movement or ignore pain, dizziness, unusual discomfort or other concerning symptoms. Stop the practice and seek appropriate medical advice when necessary.

Frequently Asked Questions

Prenatal & Postnatal Yoga FAQs

Is prenatal yoga suitable for beginners?

Many people can learn appropriately adapted prenatal yoga without previous yoga experience. The practice should be comfortable and suitable for the individual's pregnancy and health circumstances.

Can I practice yoga during pregnancy?

Pregnancy is different for every person. If your healthcare professional has cleared you for exercise, appropriately modified prenatal yoga may be considered. Always follow your healthcare provider's advice.

Can I practice the same yoga routine throughout pregnancy?

Not necessarily. As pregnancy progresses, balance, mobility, comfort and energy can change. A responsible practice should be adapted to the individual's stage and circumstances.

When can I start postnatal yoga?

The appropriate time varies depending on the individual, type of birth, recovery and medical circumstances. It is best to discuss returning to exercise with your healthcare professional before beginning.

Can postnatal yoga help me get back into exercise?

A gradual yoga routine may help rebuild movement, mobility, strength and body awareness as recovery progresses. The pace should be individual rather than focused on rushing back to intense exercise.

Do I need previous yoga experience?

No. Beginner-friendly guidance is available, and sessions can be adapted according to your previous experience and current comfort.

Can international clients join online classes?

Yes. Online sessions are designed so students can practice from home regardless of their country. Scheduling can be discussed according to your local time zone.

Are these classes a replacement for medical care?

No. Yoga classes provide wellness and movement guidance and do not replace medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always follow the recommendations of your healthcare professional.
