Before You Book

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need previous yoga experience?

No. Beginners are welcome. Sessions can be adapted according to your current level and comfort.

Are sessions available for international students?

Yes. Online yoga sessions can be conducted for students worldwide. You can mention your country and preferred time zone in the enquiry form.

Can I choose a personalized program?

Yes. Personal 1-to-1 sessions can be structured around your goals, experience and lifestyle.

How do I know which program is suitable for me?

If you are unsure, select “Not Sure — Need Guidance” in the form and describe your goals. We can discuss the appropriate direction with you.

How does online yoga work?

Sessions are conducted online using a video-call platform. You need a suitable space, comfortable clothing and a stable internet connection.

Can yoga replace medical treatment?

Yoga is intended as a wellness and educational practice and should not be considered a replacement for medical diagnosis or treatment. If you have a medical condition, injury or pregnancy-related concern, follow guidance from your qualified healthcare professional.
