Frequently Asked Questions

A few important details before your first session.

Is the first yoga session really free?

Yes. Your first introductory/demo session is complimentary. It gives us an opportunity to discuss your goals, experience and expectations before you decide whether to continue.

Do I have to continue after the free session?

No. There is no obligation to continue. If you would like to continue your yoga journey with Mukesh, paid one-to-one classes and programmes will be available.

Are the yoga classes suitable for beginners?

Yes. Beginners are welcome. Your practice can be adapted according to your experience and individual goals.

How are the sessions conducted?

Sessions are conducted online, allowing you to practise from the comfort of your home.

What is the cancellation policy?

Please provide at least 48 hours' notice if you need to cancel or reschedule a session. Late cancellations or missed sessions may not qualify for a replacement session. Emergency situations will be considered fairly.

Is yoga a replacement for medical treatment?

No. Yoga is intended to support general wellbeing and should not be considered a substitute for medical diagnosis or treatment. If you have a medical condition, injury, are pregnant, or have specific health concerns, consult an appropriate healthcare professional before beginning a new exercise or yoga programme.
