Your Practice, Your Pace

Online one-to-one guidance for people who prefer a more focused and personal yoga experience.

  • Individual attention during practice
  • Guidance suited to your experience level
  • Options to discuss your goals and schedule
  • Practice from the comfort of home
Why Choose One-to-One?

Yoga Guidance That Focuses on You

Personal sessions give you an opportunity to discuss your needs and learn with closer attention than a general group class.

🎯

Goal-Focused Practice

Discuss priorities such as building a consistent routine, improving mobility, developing strength or learning foundational yoga.

🌿

Suitable Progression

Work progressively according to your experience, comfort and current ability instead of trying to keep up with a group.

🌍

Online Convenience

Join from home. Session timing can be discussed according to availability and your time zone.

Yoga Asana

🌬️

Pranayama

🕉️

Meditation

Getting Started
