Beginner Yoga Foundation

Build Your Foundation Before You Progress

The goal of beginner yoga is not to force difficult poses. It is to understand the fundamentals, improve body awareness and develop a regular practice.

With proper guidance, you can gradually learn how to coordinate movement, breathing and awareness in a comfortable and progressive way.

  • Basic yoga postures and alignment
  • Gentle mobility and warm-up exercises
  • Flexibility and movement practice
  • Beginner-friendly Surya Namaskar
  • Basic breathing awareness
  • Relaxation and meditation

Frequently Asked Questions

Beginner Yoga FAQ

Can I start yoga if I have never done it before?

Yes. Beginner yoga is designed to introduce the fundamental movements and practices gradually. You do not need previous yoga experience.

Do I need to be flexible to start yoga?

No. Flexibility is not a requirement for beginning yoga. Regular and appropriate practice can gradually improve mobility and flexibility.

Can I learn yoga from home?

Yes. Online yoga classes allow you to practice from home with guidance. A yoga mat and enough comfortable space are usually sufficient.

Is beginner yoga suitable for older adults?

Yoga can often be adapted for different ages and abilities. However, individual health conditions and physical limitations should be considered before starting a new exercise program.

Can yoga help with weight loss?

Yoga can support an active and healthy lifestyle, but sustainable weight loss generally depends on overall energy balance, nutrition, physical activity, sleep and other lifestyle factors.

Can beginners learn pranayama?

Beginners can learn appropriate breathing awareness and suitable pranayama practices gradually. More advanced breathing techniques should be learned with proper guidance.

Can I join personal 1-to-1 beginner yoga classes?

Yes. Personal online yoga sessions can provide individual attention and allow the practice to progress according to your current ability and goals.
